# Changelog - Scorebug Studio Stream Deck plugin

Newest first. Needs Stream Deck 7.1 or newer.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-16
### Added
- **Banner keys** with ready-made messages for every sport (Touchdown, Three pointer, Home run, Goal, Yellow card, Final and more) plus Custom text.
- Banner color choice: generic (scorebug accent), home team color, away team color, or a custom color. `{T}` in a message becomes the team's name.
- Key faces show the banner color, HOME or AWAY, and "tap to hide" while on the air.
### Fixed
- `{T}` in banner text was sent literally instead of as the team's name.
- Banner keys made with earlier versions keep working unchanged.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-05
### Added
- **Period Label** key: shows HALF, OT or FINAL in place of the quarter (football, basketball).
- **Soccer Card** keys: yellow and red card for each team (tap adds, hold removes).
- **Penalty Shootout** keys: goal, miss, undo and show/hide (1 s hold clears the shootout).

## 1.0.0
- Score, game clock, show/hide, banner, period, possession, timeout, counter, base runner and game command keys.
